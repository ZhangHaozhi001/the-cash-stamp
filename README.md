# The Cash Stamp — One Coin's Day Around the World

A 5-panel AI-assisted poster comic following one Bitcoin Cash (BCH) coin through a single day:
coffee in **Tokyo**, market day in **Lagos**, a remittance home to **Manila**, a tip in
**Buenos Aires** — and a sunset over the whole planet.

**Slogan: Cash, Unstopped.**

An entry to [BCH BLAZE 2026](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/bchblaze2026/detail) — Media Track.
AI-created/assisted art is explicitly allowed in the Media Track; the series was generated with
AI image tools, directed and curated by a human, with hand-written captions and page.

Open `index.html` to view the series.
