Panel 01 · Tokyo, Japan

First stop: coffee

07:00 — the day begins

The sun is barely up, the blossoms are out, and our coin is already at work. One tap at a Tokyo coffee stand — paid in seconds. No card, no account, no “processing”. Cash doesn't queue, and neither does the coffee.

Panel 02 · Lagos, Nigeria

Market day

12:30 — halfway round the world

By midday the same coin is in a Lagos market, swapping itself for bananas and oranges. The fruit is fresh, the change is exact, and the fee is a fraction of a cent — small enough that a market stall can actually keep what it earns.

Panel 03 · Manila, Philippines

The money comes home

16:45 — same afternoon

Sent from the other side of the world, earned the hard way — and home before dinner. Remittances are lifelines, and lifelines shouldn't take three business days or lose a tenth of themselves on the way. Our coin lands at the front door, whole.

Panel 04 · Buenos Aires, Argentina

Tip the music

The tango is dancing, the guitarist is smiling, and a tip drops straight into the case — no platform in the middle taking its cut first. When paying is effortless, generosity is too. Art deserves instant applause.

Artist Statement

The Cash Stamp is a five-panel poster comic about the simplest, most radical idea in crypto: money that just works, for ordinary people, in ordinary moments — a coffee, a market stall, a family's remittance, a musician's tip.

Every stamp on a passport says you were here. Our coin collects a different kind of stamp: proof that value moved — instantly, cheaply, and without asking anyone's permission — in Tokyo, Lagos, Manila and Buenos Aires, all before the day was done.

On the making: this series was created with AI image generation, directed, curated and composed by a human artist — AI-assisted art is expressly welcomed in the BCH BLAZE Media Track, and we're proud to say so openly. One consistent coin character was carried across all five panels; captions, layout and this page were built by hand for the hackathon.

Why it matters: most people will never read a whitepaper — but everyone understands a day in the life of a coin. If this little traveller made you feel how cash without borders could work, it did its job. Fees under a cent, payments in seconds, no account required: that's Bitcoin Cash, and that's why the coin never stops.
